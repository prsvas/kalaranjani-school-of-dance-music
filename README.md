# Kalaranjani School of Dance & Music

Website for **Kalaranjani School of Dance & Music**, Hyderabad / Secunderabad.

## Included in this version
- Guru profile for **Smt. Shyamala Srinivas** based on the supplied profile document.
- Academic, artistic and honour profile.
- Performance archive organised from the supplied performance list.
- 26 supplied photographs incorporated into the Guru and student gallery.
- 12 YouTube performance links supplied in the document, presented as a video archive.
- Bharatanatyam training overview.
- Cultural programme section.
- Examination pathway placeholder pending verified university / examination-board names.
- WhatsApp contact numbers and current Kalaranjani email supplied by the institution.

## Important
The source document says many students have performed and won accolades/awards, but it does **not** provide individual student names or award details. No student names or awards have therefore been invented on the website.

The source document also does not specify the universities / examination boards used for student examinations. Add only verified affiliations to that section.
