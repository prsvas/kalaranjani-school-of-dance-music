OUR VISION

Preserving an art form.
Inspiring a generation.

Kalaranjani School of Dance & Music is dedicated to nurturing Bharatanatyam and Indian classical arts through disciplined training, meaningful performance opportunities and a strong connection to tradition.

From a first adavu to the stage, examinations and advanced performance preparation, every student is guided with care and purpose.

BHARATANATYAM TRAINING

Structured learning for every stage.

Individual attention, disciplined practice and progressive learning.

01

Beginners

Posture, basic adavus, hand gestures, rhythm and movement vocabulary.

02

Intermediate

Expanded adavus, jathis, repertoire, musical understanding and abhinaya.

03

Advanced

Advanced repertoire, expression, stage craft and performance refinement.

04

Arangetram

Long-term preparation, repertoire planning, practice discipline and stage guidance.
